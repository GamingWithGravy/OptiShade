# Contributions and licensing

Submit only code and assets you have the right to contribute. Identify copied or
adapted material, its source and its compatible licence; preserve required
notices. Do not submit confidential material or third-party code without the
necessary permissions.

- Contributions to GPL-covered runtime areas use the applicable GPL terms.
- Contributions to ReShade/BSD areas must comply with their applicable licences.
- Contributions to any explicitly designated proprietary standalone OptiShade
  component require a recorded, explicit agreement before acceptance. That
  agreement must grant gravy / GamingWithGravy the necessary rights to use,
  modify, reproduce, distribute and incorporate the contribution into that
  proprietary component under its stated licence.
- Contributors retain copyright and any rights not expressly granted or assigned.
  Submitting a change does not silently assign copyright or constitute agreement
  to proprietary use. Any assignment requires a separate express agreement.

There are currently no proprietary-designated source files; see the register in
[OPTISHADE_LICENSING.md](OPTISHADE_LICENSING.md). Do not assume that all manager
files, tests or assets are proprietary, or that a date alone changes a licence.
Review ownership, provenance, dependencies and legal separability before adding
any designation. If uncertain, preserve the existing licence and record the issue.

These rules do not withdraw permissions for older contributions or allow
third-party GPL/BSD obligations to be overridden. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)
for the component map and [BRANDING.md](BRANDING.md) for official identity rules.
