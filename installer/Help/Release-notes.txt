OptiShade 0.20.12

- Fixed an issue where changing your menu key binding could cause aircraft controls to glitch when opening or closing the UI.
- Updated Preset HotSwap: choose Preset 1 and Preset 2, and set your Preset hotswap key. An on-screen message now shows which preset is active after swapping.

Updating: Close MSFS, run the 0.20.12 installer, select your simulator and click Repair OptiShade. No restore or uninstall is needed. Restart MSFS afterwards.
