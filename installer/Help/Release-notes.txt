OptiShade 0.20.11

New Feature Added: Preset HotSwap

Uses:
- Quickly compare two different presets.
- Switch between day and night presets while flying.
- Keep two favourites ready to swap whenever you cannot decide.

How to use:
1. Open the OptiShade UI and go to Image effects.
2. Choose a Main Preset and a different Hotswap Preset.
3. Click Change beside Hotswap and press your preferred key.
4. Save settings.
5. Close the UI and swap as you wish!

Save or revert unsaved preset edits before swapping. Different shaders may briefly reload.

Updating: Close MSFS, run the 0.20.11 installer, select your simulator and click Repair OptiShade. No restore or uninstall is needed. Restart MSFS afterwards.
