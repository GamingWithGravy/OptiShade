Back up anything important before testing. Restore and Uninstall are designed to remove OptiShade's tracked files and restore its backups without changing game settings, controls or saves. Restore keeps saved INI presets in the game presets folder. Uninstall asks whether to keep or remove those presets. Presets saved elsewhere are left alone; downloaded effects are removed.
